One valuation project: was Netflix's offer for Warner Bros. Discovery worth what it proposed to pay?

Project

Netflix and Warner Bros. Discovery acquisition valuation

I tested whether Netflix's proposed offer of $27.75 per share for Warner Bros. Discovery (WBD) was justified. I built a sum-of-the-parts discounted cash flow (DCF) model in Excel. It values each WBD business segment separately and adds them up to estimate the whole company.

I built bear, base and bull scenarios to see how much the answer moves when key assumptions change. I also looked at the regulatory and debt risks of the deal.

MeasureResult
Netflix offer$27.75 per share
Valuation range, bear to bull$18.18 to $38.55 per share

About me

I'm Vraj Patel, a finance and international business student at Simon Fraser University. I want to understand how markets, companies and technology change, and I use data to find out why.

AI is now part of finance and data analysis, from how trades are made to how research gets done. I use it in my own work. For my Aritzia research I built an AI-assisted workflow to collect Reddit comments, then checked the coding by hand. I think new tools only help when people understand them, can trust them and share in the benefits. That is why I support innovation that comes with social awareness, acceptance and education.

My coursework is built on real data: company valuation, portfolio optimization and market research, with models in Excel that I test against what happened. I'm also building my skills in Power BI and SQL so I can turn larger data sets into dashboards and answers. Outside class I work at H&M in Vancouver and volunteered as a Peer Educator at Fraser International College, where I helped more than 100 new international students settle in.

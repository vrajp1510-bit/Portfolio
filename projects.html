Ascend SFU · Accounting Club

Embark Sustainability

Financial sustainability and revenue strategy for a student-run, not-for-profit society.

87–96%of revenue from SFU fees
$64,442deficit in 2023
~19%modelled revenue gain

Embark Sustainability Society is a student-run, not-for-profit sustainability organization at SFU. The case asked how Embark could stay financially healthy if its SFU funding fell by about 60%, while keeping programs affordable for students and true to its mission.

We read five years of audited statements (2016, 2017 and 2022 to 2024). Membership fees collected by SFU made up 87% to 96% of revenue in every year, so the organization depended almost entirely on one source. Wages rose 31% in 2023, which pushed expenses to $339,472 and created a $64,442 deficit. Cash fell by a third that year, from $174,230 to $116,155.

The board has held a $20,000 operating reserve since 2019. Because it is set aside as a buffer, we treated it as restricted, not as cash for day-to-day spending. It covers less than one month of 2024 expenses.

Our recommendation paired a revised fee structure with more varied funding: grants, partnerships, donations and other revenue activities. We modelled the fee changes at roughly a 19% revenue gain, about $55,600 on 2024 revenue of $292,725, while protecting student affordability.

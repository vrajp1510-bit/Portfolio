A netnography of Aritzia: what consumers say about a brand when they talk to each other online.

Project

Netnography market research: Aritzia

Netnography studies what people say in online communities to understand how they feel about a brand. I designed an AI-assisted research workflow using Claude and the Apify Reddit Scraper connector to collect real consumer comments from Reddit. Every comment stays traceable to its source thread.

  • I organized the data in a multi-tab Excel workbook and coded the comments into themes.
  • For each theme I summarized how often it came up and whether the sentiment was positive or negative.
  • I checked the AI-generated coding myself for accuracy.
  • I then applied Hofstede's cultural dimensions to turn the findings into brand positioning recommendations.
MeasureResult
Comments coded135 from 3 subreddits (r/PetiteFashionAdvice, r/femalefashionadvice, r/Anticonsumption)
Themes10, led by Fit and Sizing (24%), Quality and Durability (16%) and Competitor Comparison (13%)
Sentiment31% positive, 25% mixed, 24% negative, 19% neutral
ToolsClaude, Apify Reddit Scraper, Excel

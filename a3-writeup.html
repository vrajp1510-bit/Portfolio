The limitations of the minimum variance weights are that the hedge is based on historical data which correlations which can shift sharply. Another limitation is that it doesn’t account for the transaction costs involved with shorting which could significantly affect the calculations.

The qualitative adjustment that I would make is to reduce the short position in QQQ. This will increase the variance, but it will also make the hedge less concentrated in large-cap technology and therefore more diversified and more robust if tech-sector correlations change.

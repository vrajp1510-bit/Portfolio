Business and strategy

  • Good to GreatJim Collins

    Good to Great

    Jim Collins

    Jim Collins and his research team compare companies that made a lasting leap in results with similar companies that did not, and describe what the leaders and cultures behind the leap had in common.

  • Zero to OnePeter Thiel

    Zero to One

    Peter Thiel

    Peter Thiel argues that real progress comes from creating something new, not copying what already works. It grew out of his Stanford startup course and was written with Blake Masters.

  • The China PriceAlexandra Harney

    The China Price

    Alexandra Harney

    A reporter's look inside Chinese factories at the human and environmental costs behind the low prices of goods made for export.

Personal money habits first, then how modern markets work.

Money and markets

  • The Richest Man in BabylonGeorge S. Clason

    The Richest Man in Babylon

    George S. Clason

    Short parables set in ancient Babylon that teach plain money habits: pay yourself first, live on less than you earn, make your savings earn more, and guard them from risky schemes. First published in 1926.

  • Trading at the Speed of LightDonald MacKenzie

    Trading at the Speed of Light: How Ultrafast Algorithms Are Transforming Financial Markets

    Donald MacKenzie

    A sociologist's study, built on interviews with traders, of how high-frequency trading firms compete on speed and what that means for how financial markets work.

Dealing with others, and judging them well.

People and conduct

  • How to Win Friends and Influence PeopleDale Carnegie

    How to Win Friends and Influence People

    Dale Carnegie

    First published in 1936, it sets out simple principles for dealing with people: take a real interest in others, avoid criticizing, and see things from the other person's side.

  • Chanakya NitiChanakya

    Chanakya Niti

    Chanakya

    Maxims on conduct, work and governing attributed to Chanakya, the adviser who served Chandragupta Maurya. They deal with choosing friends carefully, discipline, patience and judging people by what they do.

Duty, strength, a reason to get up, and how the brain works.

Mind and purpose

  • Bhagavad Gita

    Bhagavad Gita

    A Sanskrit dialogue from the Mahabharata in which the prince Arjuna asks Krishna about duty, action and acting without attachment to the result.

  • Swami Vivekananda: Motivational ThoughtsSwami Vivekananda

    Swami Vivekananda: Motivational Thoughts

    Swami Vivekananda

    A collection of sayings and talks on strength, self-belief, discipline and service. Vivekananda's core message is that people should trust their own power and keep working toward a goal without fear.

  • IkigaiHéctor García, Francesc Miralles

    Ikigai

    Héctor García, Francesc Miralles

    Draws on the authors' time in Ogimi, Japan, where many people live past 100, to look at how a sense of purpose, small daily routines and close friendships shape a long and satisfying life. The idea of ikigai is the overlap of what you love, what you are good at, what the world needs and what you can be paid for.

  • Phantoms in the BrainV. S. Ramachandran, Sandra Blakeslee

    Phantoms in the Brain: Probing the Mysteries of the Human Mind

    V. S. Ramachandran, Sandra Blakeslee

    The neurologist V. S. Ramachandran describes patients with unusual brain conditions, such as phantom limbs, and uses their cases to show how the brain builds our sense of the body, perception and the self.
